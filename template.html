What is Nexor

One currency. Real money behind it.

Nexor is the digital currency of LuckieLynx. It will be backed by real money, so what you win in a match is worth something outside the game too.

  1. Buy Nexor.Top up your account with money and get Nexor in return.
  2. Play with it.Match entries are paid in Nexor, and everything you win is paid in Nexor.
  3. Cash out.Sell the Nexor you've collected back for money whenever you want.

The Nexor economy is still being built. Exchange rates and payment methods will be announced before launch.

How a match works

From the entry fee to the last train out.

Every match follows the same run. Ride in, get your stake, survive, and make it back before the doors close.

  1. Pay the entry

    Pay the match entry in Nexor to join a lobby. Play solo or with a squad, depending on the mode.

  2. Ride in by train

    When the lobby fills, everyone rides in on the same train heading north to the outpost. Jump off wherever you want.

  3. Run for the bank

    Your stake, your entry minus the fee, is waiting in the East or West bank at opposite ends of the map. Gear up on the way.

  4. Hold it or take more

    Once you collect your Nexor, you're a target. Take out other players and their Nexor becomes yours.

  5. Make the train

    The train waits at the outpost until time runs out. Get on board and everything you carry is secured.

  6. Split the pool

    Nexor that doesn't leave on the train goes into a pool, shared only among players who made it, by match points.

Game mode

Nexor Extraction. Get your stake. Get out.

A battle royale built around a train. You don't need to be the last one standing. You need to be on board when it leaves.

  • Two banks, one is live. Each match, the Nexor goes to either the East or the West bank, far from where you drop.

  • Your stake is your entry. Enter for 6 Nexor, and after a 2 Nexor fee you collect 4 from the bank.

  • Carrying it makes you a target. Anyone you knock out drops their Nexor to you. Anyone can do the same to you.

  • Miss the train, lose it. The train leaves on time. Whatever isn't on it goes into the pool for the players on board.

Where the Nexor goes

Make the train. Split the pool.

Getting on the train pays twice. You keep what you carry, and you get a share of everything left behind.

  • On the train: yours.Everything you carry on board is secured, including Nexor you took from other players.
  • Left behind: the pool.Nexor still sitting in the bank, plus Nexor from players who didn't make the train, goes into one pool.
  • Split among the players on board.LuckieLynx takes a small percentage of the pool. The rest goes only to players who made the train, by the points they earned. More points, bigger share.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is LuckieLynx?

LuckieLynx is a skill-based play-to-earn game in development. Its main mode, Nexor Extraction, is a battle royale built around a train: you pay an entry in Nexor, ride in with the lobby, collect your stake from the bank and get back on the train before it leaves. Whatever you carry on board is yours.

What is Nexor?

Nexor is the digital currency of LuckieLynx. You buy Nexor, use it to pay match entries and win more of it in matches. Nexor will be backed by real money, so you can cash out the Nexor you collect.

Is it down to luck?

No. There are no dice, cards or slot machines. What you walk away with depends on how you play: the fights you win, the routes you take and when you decide to run for the train.

What happens to Nexor that misses the train?

It goes into a pool. LuckieLynx takes a small percentage, and the rest is split between the players who made the train, based on the points they earned in the match.

When can I play?

LuckieLynx is in development. Create an account to reserve your username, and we'll email you when the alpha opens if you ask us to.

Do I need to be 18?

Yes. You need to be 18 or older to create a LuckieLynx account.
